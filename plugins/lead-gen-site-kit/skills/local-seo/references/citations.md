# Citations and social profiles

Citations are listings of the business name, phone and details on other sites. Consistent citations on trusted directories reinforce the Google profile and the site's schema, add prominence, and often send referral traffic and a link. Social profiles make the brand an entity that search and AI engines recognise.

## The golden rule: identical details everywhere

Use the master details block, character for character, on every listing. Inconsistent name, phone or address is the one off-page action that actively hurts local rankings rather than merely wasting time.

**Prerequisites before submitting anything:** the master details block is final, the phone is real, and the business number (ABN) is published on the site. Directories ask for it.

## Tiers (Australian examples, as used on previous builds)

Directory quality changes over time. Confirm each is live and reputable at signup. Translate the tiers to the equivalent platforms in other countries.

**Tier 0, owner controlled (free, do first):** Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Facebook Page.

**Tier 1, core directories:** Yellow Pages, White Pages, True Local, Hotfrog, Yelp, Localsearch, Word of Mouth, ProductReview.com.au, Brownbook, AussieWeb, dLook.

**Tier 2, niche marketplaces (often also paid lead sources):** for home services, hipages, Airtasker, ServiceSeeking, Bark. Many require the account holder to do the work; lead networks check eligibility first. Decide case by case whether to pay for leads.

**Tier 3, social profiles:** Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn company page, YouTube, TikTok, Pinterest, Nextdoor. Use one handle everywhere it fits. Brand them consistently and post real before and after content.

**Tier 4, local and civic:** chamber of commerce member directories, local business associations, council business directories.

## Process

1. Lock the master details block.
2. Do Tier 0 first, then batch Tier 1 a few at a time.
3. Record every listing in a tracker: platform, URL, status, date, details confirmed (yes or no), where the login lives (never the password).
4. When a social or directory profile goes live, add its URL to `SITE.sameAs` in the site's data file so the Organization schema references it. Only live, resolving URLs.
5. Recheck a sample of listings each quarter, and every listing whenever a detail changes.

## Skip

- **Directory blast services.** Many listed directories are dead or irrelevant; the result is dead links and inconsistent details.
- Directories that charge for nothing of value, and anything that looks like a link farm.
- Listings in cities where the business has no real presence.
