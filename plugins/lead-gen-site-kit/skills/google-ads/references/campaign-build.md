# Building the search campaign

Produce every part as a table the user can copy into the account or an import sheet.

## Structure

- One search campaign per site.
- One ad group per service. Add an ad group per location only where a location has its own page and enough searches to justify it.
- Each ad group's keywords, ads and landing page are about the same thing. The landing page is the matching service or location page, never the home page by default.

## Keywords

- Up to 10 per ad group, from the keyword map, in phrase and exact match. Use as many as the map supports. A tight ad group of three real keywords is better than ten padded ones.
- Keep searches that show someone wants the service now, in the area: the service, the service with the city or suburb, "near me", emergency and quote searches.
- Leave out research, do-it-yourself and job-seeker searches.

## Negative keywords

A campaign-level list of at least 50 terms, grouped so the user can read it:

- Do it yourself: how to, diy, tutorial, guide, video, instructions
- Jobs and training: jobs, careers, salary, course, apprenticeship, training, hiring
- Intent the buyer does not serve: second hand, used, hire, rental (only where it does not apply). Do not add "free" if the call to action offers a free quote
- Products rather than services: parts, supplies, wholesale, for sale, buy, kit
- Other places: every major city and region outside the service area
- Other services the buyer does not offer, taken from the brief
- Competitor and brand names, unless the user chooses to bid on them

Never add a negative that appears in the user's own keywords, call to action or service list. Ask the user to read the list before loading it. A negative that matches a real service blocks real leads.

## Ads

For each ad group, a responsive search ad:

- 12 headlines, each within 30 characters. Include the service, the place, the main action in its exact wording from the brief, and true reasons to choose the business. Vary them; do not repeat one idea twelve times.
- 4 descriptions, each within 90 characters, with what happens next and one proof point from the brief.
- Count the characters and show the count. Rewrite anything over the limit.

## Extras

- Call: the tracking phone number, scheduled to the hours it is answered. Skip it until the number exists.
- Links to the other services, each with a short description.
- Short benefit phrases, true ones, from the brief.
- A location extra only where the business has a verified profile.

## Before handing over

- Check every claim in every headline and description against the brief. Remove anything unsourced.
- For a lead network, make sure no ad says or implies the brand does the work itself.
- List what the user must supply or decide: the daily cap, the maximum click cost, the schedule, the service area.
